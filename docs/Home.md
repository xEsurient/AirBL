# Welcome to the AirBL Wiki!

**AirBL** is your all-in-one network engine for monitoring, verifying, and routing AirVPN connections. Designed initially to circumvent DroneBL bans, it has evolved into a robust connection orchestrator for Docker and bare-metal environments.

Whether you're struggling with Captchas on blacklisted endpoints, chasing the lowest latency Entry IP, or trying to feed reliable configurations to your Gluetun container, AirBL handles the entire pipeline internally.
